1“是”字结构

处理：「是」= be (is/am/are/was/were) + 名词/形容词。多用于「S + be + 表语」。也可译为 serve as / represent / act as（当"是"表"作为/象征"时）。注意与第 5 节"之一"、第 2 节"的"配合。
中国是文明古国，拥有悠久历史。
China is an ancient civilization with a long history.
爆竹（firecrackers）是中国传统文化中的重要元素之一。它在中国有悠久的历史。
Firecrackers are one of the important elements in traditional Chinese culture. They have a long history in China.
重庆是一座山城，备受游客喜爱。
Chongqing is a mountain city, deeply loved by tourists.
我是一名大学生，主修人文学科。
I am a college student, majoring in humanities.

2“的”字结构

处理：「的」= 英语的 of 短语或前置定语，按类型转换：
  • 类型一（修饰语 + 的 + N）：译为 "N + of + 修饰语" 或前置形容词。
  • 类型二（形容词 + 的 + N）：直接前置形容词。
  • 类型三（动宾/被动 + 的 + N）：译为 分词作定语（loved / gained / received / criticized）或定语从句。
  • 类型四（有着…的 + N）：译为 "N + with + 名词"。
中国文化的一个重要元素
an important element of Chinese culture
环保意识的增强
the enhancement of environmental awareness
爆竹的使用
the use of firecrackers
悠久的历史
a long history
灿烂美丽的文化
a brilliant and beautiful culture
高度发达的产业
a highly developed industry
受人们喜爱的小说人物
a fictional character loved by people
获得财富的小男孩
the little boy who gained wealth
收到礼物的小朋友
the child who received a gift
被父母批评的孩子
the child criticized by his parents
有着花园的房子
a house with a garden
对婚姻有着偏见的人们
people with prejudice against marriage

3“有”字结构

处理：「有」三种译法：
  • 类型一（拥有）：have。
  • 类型二（存在，无明确所属）：There be 句型。
  • 类型三（伴随/附带，作补充说明）：with + 名词 短语。
中国有 14 亿人口。
China has a population of 1.4 billion.
唐代诗人有许多著作。
Poets of the Tang Dynasty had many works.
在美国，有高度发达的芯片技术。
In the US, there is highly developed chip technology.
2008 年有一场地震。
In 2008, there was an earthquake.
他是一名著名的主持人，有着很多粉丝。
He is a famous host with many fans.
中国十分重视环境保护，有许多相关的政策。
China attaches great importance to environmental protection, with many related policies.

4“随着”结构

处理：「随着」= With + 名词短语（把动词名词化），或 As + 从句（保留动词）。
  • 类型一（随着 + 名词）：With + N。
  • 类型二（随着 + 句子）：As + 从句。
随着环保意识的增强
With the enhancement of environmental awareness
随着人们兴趣的增加
With the increase of people's interest
随着收入的减少
With the decrease of income
随着人们对中国传统文化越来越感兴趣
As people become increasingly interested in traditional Chinese culture
随着当地政府开始重视人们的生活质量
As the local government begins to value people's quality of life

5“之一”结构

处理：「…之一」= one of + 复数名词。注意 one of 后必须接复数，且前面动词用单数。
重庆大学是众多高等院校之一。
Chongqing University is one of the many institutions of higher education.
山东是中国众多省份之一。
Shandong is one of China's many provinces.
国画是中国文化艺术种类之一。
Traditional Chinese painting is one of the categories of Chinese culture and art.
中国传统文化中的重要元素之一。
one of the important elements in traditional Chinese culture.

6句中无动词处理方法

处理：中文"名词 + 形容词"可无动词，英文必须补 be 动词（is/are），或用 with 结构，或转化为 "has + 名词"。
中国幅员辽阔，人口众多。
China has a vast territory and a large population.
或 China is vast in territory and large in population.
海南雨水丰富，阳光充足，生物多样。
Hainan is rich in rainfall, abundant in sunshine, and biodiverse.
这部电影粉丝很多，口碑很好。
This film has many fans and a good reputation.

7句中无主语的处理方法

处理：三选一——①补泛指主语 people / we / one；②改被动语态（最常用，尤其"建立/点燃"类）；③用 There be / It is + 过去分词 + that。
人们认为，通过这么做，可以驱散厄运，带来好运吉祥。
People believe that by doing so, bad luck can be dispelled and good fortune brought.
战后改变了世界格局。
The post-war period changed the world pattern.
或 The world pattern was changed after the war.
通过政府引导和社会参与，建立更多的养老机构。
Through government guidance and social participation, more elderly care institutions should be established.
灯笼内部点燃蜡烛。
Candles are lit inside the lanterns.

8长名词的处理方法

处理：中文长名词串（N1N2N3…）最后一个是中心词，前面都是修饰。译为 "中心词 + of + 修饰" 或转化为 "adj + 中心词"；并列名词用 and 连接。
农业生产活动
agricultural production activities
经济文化社会发展
economic, cultural and social development
社会实践活动
social practice activities
中国传统绘画历史十分悠久。
The history of traditional Chinese painting is very long.
或 Traditional Chinese painting has a very long history.

9两大必考时态

处理：
  • 一般过去时：有明确过去时间（唐代 / 1999 年 / 古代）→ 动词用过去式。
  • 现在完成时 / 完成进行时：含"一直 / 自…以来 / 过去 N 年"→ have/has done；被动完成用 have/has been done。
唐代，经济繁荣，人们生活幸福，进出口产业发达。
In the Tang Dynasty, the economy was prosperous, people lived happily, and the import-export industry was developed.
1999 年，世界经历了巨大变化。
In 1999, the world experienced tremendous changes.
在古代，人们崇尚尊师重道。
In ancient times, people valued respecting teachers and honoring the way.
他已经被说服去洗碗了。
He has been persuaded to do the dishes.
中国一直践行绿色发展的方针。
China has always practiced the policy of green development.
自 2000 年以来，中国驶入经济发展快车道。
Since 2000, China has entered a fast lane of economic development.
在过去 20 年里，他饱经沧桑，阅人无数，深谙人生之道。
In the past 20 years, he has experienced many vicissitudes, met countless people, and deeply understood the way of life.

10隐藏的被动关系

处理：中文常用主动形式表达被动含义（"可采摘 / 种植 / 确定 / 制定"）。英文必须显化 be + 过去分词。
铁观音一年四季均可采摘。
Tieguanyin can be picked all year round.
粮食一般在春天种植，秋天收获，冬天储存。
Grain is generally planted in spring, harvested in autumn, and stored in winter.
你的行程确定后，一定要告诉我。
After your itinerary is confirmed, be sure to tell me.
新规则制定后，大家一定要遵守。
After the new rules are formulated, everyone must obey them.

11双动词如何处理

处理：一句两动词，常用：① and 连接两个并列谓语；② 第二个动词变 分词（V-ing 表伴随 / V-ed 表被动）；③ 转化为 "to do" 表目的。
他飞越了大半个地球，去到了梦想中的地方。
He flew across most of the globe and arrived at the place of his dreams.
或 …to reach the place of his dreams.
她接听了电话，变得十分沮丧。
She answered the phone and became very depressed.
熊猫是十分可爱的动物，深受世界各地的人们喜爱。
Pandas are very lovely animals, deeply loved by people around the world.
他们跟你建立了深厚的感情，不希望你离开。
They have built deep affection with you and don't want you to leave.

12定语从句的使用

处理：
  • 类型一（逗号隔开，修饰前面名词）：非限制性定语从句，用 which / who。
  • 类型二（"的"后接宾语，从句缺宾语）：限制性定语从句 that / which（作宾语可省略）。
  • 类型三（which 指代前面整句话）：非限制性 which 指代整个主句。
我喜欢这枚闪闪发光的戒指，它真的很漂亮。
I like this sparkling ring, which is really beautiful.
人总是不愿意遵守规则，规则最终也被人们打破。
People are always unwilling to obey rules, which are eventually broken by people.
中国有很多艺术家，他们十分有才华。
China has many artists, who are very talented.
中国有很多艺术家，他们中的大部分有才华。
China has many artists, most of whom are talented.
他从不吃他讨厌的食物。
He never eats the food (that) he hates.
Jacky 从来不听别人指出的缺点。
Jacky never listens to the shortcomings (that) others point out.
你应该多锻炼，这对你很重要。
You should exercise more, which is very important to you.
凡人多舛误，唯神能见宥，这是十分正确的。
Mortals often err and only gods can forgive, which is very correct.
中国坚持在保护环境中发展，在发展中保护环境，这获得了大多数国家的认可。
China insists on developing while protecting the environment and protecting it while developing, which has been recognized by most countries.

13语序原则

处理：中文"状语 + 中心"常见，英文遵循 主 + 谓 + 宾 +（地点/方式/时间放句尾）。特别是"在…方面"→ "in…" 放句末或句首；"通过/根据"→ "through / based on" 放句首或句尾。
中国古代的人们道德水平很高。
People in ancient China had a high moral standard.
张军在大数据方面表现十分优秀。
Zhang Jun performs excellently in big data.
它在众多中小型企业中不算突出。
It is not outstanding among many small and medium-sized enterprises.
在中国，很多人在互联网上当键盘侠。
In China, many people act as keyboard warriors on the internet.
你可以通过互联网获得很多信息。
You can obtain a lot of information through the internet.
科学家根据自然界的规律发明了很多科技。
Scientists have invented a lot of technology based on the laws of nature.

14三大动词要提后

处理：「出现 / 发生 / 存在」这类"无主句"动词，英文要把真实主语后置，用 There appeared / There occurred / There exist 或把事件作主语放前面。
宋代出现了火药爆竹。
In the Song Dynasty, gunpowder firecrackers appeared.
或 There appeared gunpowder firecrackers in the Song Dynasty.
2030 年发生了断网停电。
In 2030, a network outage and power failure occurred.
或 There occurred…
这片区域存在很多资源。
There exist many resources in this area.
或 Many resources exist in this area.

15如何对句子无效信息进行删减

处理：中文多四字格、同义反复（"彼此相互""彻底解决""息息相关"）。翻译只取核心意思，不逐字译；冗余合并或删去。
中国的房价之所以一路飙升与购房观念息息相关。
The soaring housing prices in China are closely related to the concept of home buying.
一年四季的变化。
The changes of the four seasons.
或 seasonal changes
南京长江大桥是中国设计、采用国产材料建造的铁路、公路两用桥。
The Nanjing Yangtze River Bridge is a combined railway and highway bridge designed by China and built with domestic materials.
我们要彻底解决缺水、营养不良的难题。
We must thoroughly solve the problems of water shortage and malnutrition.
我们要坚持"人民至上"的方针。
We must adhere to the principle of "people first".
它们彼此之间相互关联。
They are interconnected.
彼此相互 = 冗余
推行家庭养老和社会养老相结合的模式。
We should promote a model combining family and social elderly care.

16如何写对难度较高的单词或词组

处理：不会写难词就 paraphrase（释义/近义替换），或用简单词拆分：
  • 归属感 → sense of belonging
  • 飞跃 → leap / great progress
  • 总部 → headquarters
  • 通道 → passage / corridor / channel
  • 羊圈 → sheepfold（题目已给）
房子成为中国人有归属感的重要标志，并深入人心。
Houses have become an important symbol of Chinese people's sense of belonging and are deeply rooted in their hearts.
中国桥梁建设的一个飞跃。
a leap in China's bridge construction.
中央电视台总部设有一条穿过大楼的专用通道。
The CCTV headquarters has a dedicated passage running through the building.
夜间有狼钻进了羊圈（sheepfold）叼走了羊。
At night, a wolf got into the sheepfold and took away the sheep.

17关于数字的翻译

处理：英文无"万/亿"直接对应词，需换算：
  • 万 = ten thousand（三万 = 30,000 = thirty thousand）
  • 十万 = hundred thousand
  • 百万 = million ｜ 千万 = ten million
  • 亿 = hundred million（一亿 = 100,000,000）
  • 分数：四分之三 = three fourths / three quarters；一半 = half
300 名游客
300 tourists
三千名游客
3,000 tourists (three thousand)
三万名游客
30,000 tourists (thirty thousand)
三十万名游客
300,000 tourists (three hundred thousand)
三十三万名游客
330,000 tourists (three hundred and thirty thousand)
两百万名游客
2,000,000 tourists (two million)
两千万名游客
20,000,000 tourists (twenty million)
两千三百万名游客
23,000,000 tourists (twenty-three million)
1 亿游客
100,000,000 tourists (one hundred million)
55 亿游客
5,500,000,000 tourists (five billion five hundred million)
你需要抽出你四分之三的时间。
You need to spare three fourths of your time.
给我一半的钱。
Give me half of the money.

18关于各种时间朝代的翻译

处理：
  • 朝代：the + X + Dynasty（首字母大写）。
  • 时期：the + X + Period。
  • 公元前：3000 BC（或 B.C.E.）｜ 公元：AD / CE（通常直接写年份）。
  • 节日：the + 节日名（首字母大写）。
唐朝
the Tang Dynasty
宋朝
the Song Dynasty
元朝
the Yuan Dynasty
唐宋时期
the Tang and Song dynasties / the Tang-Song period
春秋时期
the Spring and Autumn Period
公元前 3000 年
3000 BC
公元 2009 年
2009 AD
通常直接写 2009
春节
the Spring Festival
元宵节
the Lantern Festival
2010 年
the year 2010 / in 2010

19关于河流山脉的翻译

处理：
  • 江河：the + X + River（长江/黄河需加 the；长江也可写 the Changjiang River）。
  • 山：Mount + X（单座）；山脉用 Mountains。
  • 湖：X + Lake（太湖 Taihu Lake）。
  • 海/湾：the + X + Sea / Bay（渤海 the Bohai Sea）。
长江
the Yangtze River
或 the Changjiang River
黄河
the Yellow River
珠江
the Pearl River
黄山
Mount Huang
或 Huangshan Mountain
太湖
Taihu Lake
渤海
the Bohai Sea
