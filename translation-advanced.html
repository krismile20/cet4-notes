01

数词类

8 条

数量表达最容易写「对」但不出彩，替换一次就能看出词汇量。

大量的a large number of / a large amount of / a great deal of / numerous / a vast quantity of（number 接可数，amount / deal 接不可数）
一些some / a few / several / a certain number of / a handful of
大多数the majority of / most of / the vast majority of
少部分a small proportion of / a minority of / a small percentage of
一系列a series of / a range of / a variety of / a sequence of
各种various / a variety of / all kinds of / diverse / manifold
大约approximately / around / about / roughly / an estimated + 数字订
多 / 余 / 超过more than / over / in excess of / exceeding / beyond
搭配a number of + 可数复数；an amount of / a deal of + 不可数。写成 a large number of water 立刻暴露。
考场数字前用 approximately 最稳；roughly 偏口语，in excess of 偏正式文书。

动词类

26 条

动词是句子的骨架。换一个动词，句子的「档次」立刻不同。

吸引attract / draw / appeal to / fascinate
减少reduce / decrease / diminish / cut down / decline
象征symbolize / represent / signify / stand for
影响influence / affect / impact / have an impact on
促进promote / boost / facilitate / advance / foster
积累accumulate / build up / gather / amass
形成form / take shape / shape / constitute
传递convey / pass on / transmit / deliver
涵盖cover / encompass / include / embrace
坐落于be located in / be situated in / lie in
追溯到date back to / trace back to / originate from
蕴含contain / embody / imply / hold / embrace
起源于originate from / originate in / stem from / date back to
突破break through / make a breakthrough / surpass / exceed订
保护protect / preserve / safeguard / conserve
普及popularize / spread / promote / gain popularity订

订正记录

21 条

左列是原稿（划掉的即改动处），右列是订正写法与理由。左边保留原样，方便你对照老师原话。

词条原稿订正理由
大约an estimatedan estimated + 数字estimated 是形容词，必须带所修饰的数量：an estimated 30 million
巧妙的cunningclever / ingenious / skillfulcunning 是「狡猾」，贬义，不能夸设计巧妙
古老的antiqueancient / old / age-oldantique 只形容器物古董，不修饰文明、城市
人们the massesthe public / individualsthe masses 带强烈政治色彩，翻译题里不宜用
影响repercussioninfluence / impact / effectrepercussion 多指负面后果，语义偏窄
使……renderrender + 宾语 + 形容词render 后接形容词（render sth. possible），不能直接接动词
突破surpass / exceedbreak through / make a breakthroughsurpass、exceed 是「超过、胜过」，不等于「突破」
普及universalizepopularize / gain popularityuniversalize 生僻且偏哲学术语，考试用不到
被视为be considered asbe considered / be regarded asconsider 后接 as 属冗余，规范写法是 consider sb. sth. 或 regard as
发挥重要作用make a difference inplay an important role inmake a difference 意为「有影响、起作用」，不等于「发挥重要作用」
满足需求fulfill the requirements ofmeet the needs of / satisfy the demand forrequirements 是「要求」，不是「需求」
由……制成be made up ofbe made of / from / out ofbe made up of 是「由…组成」，不是「制成」
防止……protect … fromprevent … from / guard againstprotect from 是「保护免受」，主语与宾语关系不同
享有……美誉be exalted asbe hailed as / enjoy a reputation forexalt 意为「颂扬、抬高」，不接 as 表「被誉为」
农历八月初七the 7th day of lunar Augustthe 7th day of the 8th lunar month农历月份不能说 lunar August，应是「第 8 个农历月」
祭拜worship Godsworship gods泛指的「神」小写；只有一神教的 God 才大写
文化底蕴cultural depositcultural heritage / cultural depthdeposit 是「存款、沉积物」，属直译生造
据说Word has it that（that 不可省略）It is said that / Word has it thatthat 可以省略；且考试更常用 It is said that
菜系eight major cuisineseight great cuisines固定说法是 the eight great cuisines；cuisine 作「菜系」时才可数
密切相关be closely related with / tobe closely related torelated 后固定接 to，with 不成立
历史名胜historical resortshistoric sites / places of interestresort 是度假地；historic 才是「有历史意义的」
